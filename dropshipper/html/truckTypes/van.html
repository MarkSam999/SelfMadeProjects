Van

Van is one of the types of cargo cars. This type is very diverse, but it's also the most popular choice.

Unlike the other types of cars, this type includes cars that have a wide spread of most of the parameters. For example, there are vans that can carry only a few pounds, while there are also some vans that can carry up to a ton. Or fuel consumption - another important consideration. This is because unlike the other types of cargo cars, this type includes cars that use different types of fuel, such as diesel, gas, electricity, sometimes combined, while its engine structure depends heavily on brand, model, and the year of production.

Despite that this type is not so monotonous as other types could be, this type is strongly preferred across many cargo drivers. This is primarily because they have some traits in common:

  • Huge trunks: even the smallest vans have about 200 cubic feet
  • Large load: despite having wide spread, most of them can still carry a lot
  • Class-E is enough: technically, it's considered a regular car, so it's not necessary to have something else than Class-E driver license to drive it
