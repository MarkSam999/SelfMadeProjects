Semi-Truck

Just like vans, it's also popular, but this type is completely different from others in almost all parameters. Most obviously, it's very huge, heavy, and has a thick "armor", creating a sense of this type being like a tank, but on the market.

On one hand, it makes the truck acceleration too slow and the fuel consumption too high, leading to this type being both hard to drive (CDL as a requirement is not for fun, right?) and expensive. On the other hand, it can lift a very heavy masses, and has much more chances in saving the loaded cargo, even when something goes wrong.

Based on that, semi-trucks could be great for the following:

  • Commercial: many grocery stores deliver food by using this type
  • Moving out: every citizen can rent out such type, load all domestic stuff like furniture, hardware, and tableware, and move to his/her new home
  • Class-E is enough: technically, it's considered a regular car, so it's not necessary to have something else than Class-E driver license to drive it
