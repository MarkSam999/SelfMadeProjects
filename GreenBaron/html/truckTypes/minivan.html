Minivan

Minivan is one of the types of the cargo cars. Its main feature is universality.

For the first time, it seems like it's a bus because of the much more passenger seats - about 6 seats, and the trunk being very small.

Minivan with folded seats

However, it has a unique feature - most of the seats can be folded down. As you can see at this image, when the seats are folded down, the trunk space increases significantly, so you can load more or larger cargo. Along with that, most of the minivans have almost the same length as many pickups.

Based on this information, this type fits well when:

  • You have a lot of cargo to transport
  • You need to transport both people and cargo
  • You don't have enough skills for driving something bigger, like trucks
