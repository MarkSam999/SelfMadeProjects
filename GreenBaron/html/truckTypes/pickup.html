Pickup

Pickup is one of the types of cargo cars available on our imaginary transportation service. It's main feature is the small size.

Compared to the other types of the cargo cars, this type, along with low costs and driving requirements, has low fuel consumption (about 20 miles per gallon), and accelerates very fast, usually from 3 to 6 seconds. However, its trunk is very small, while the length depends severely on the brand and model, and because of its relatively weak engine, this type cannot carry too much weight.

Conclusively, this car might be the best choice in the following cases:

  • You have Class-E Driver License only, don't have or just not sure in your driving skills
  • Your cargo is relatively small in size and lightweight
  • Your route goes through the cities or any other places with the dense traffic, overcrowded streets, or thin roads
